## Design diagnosis

### Strong points

- The lab metaphor is original and directly related to infrastructure and operations work.
- The service language is credible and concrete: Linux/Docker, observability, APIs/reconciliation, commerce and recovery.
- The scene has enough depth and object variety to support exploration.
- The fallback/readable path is better than making the portfolio WebGL-only.
- The visual system is coherent: dark surfaces, warm practical light, teal/amber signals and monospace labels.
- The layout avoids generic SaaS card-grid composition in the hero.

### Conversion risks

1. **Offer appears too late.** The first viewport is mostly scene; a visitor may understand the theme before understanding what can be hired.
2. **No primary contact CTA.** GitHub/Workana in the footer are not a clear project-start action.
3. **Hotspots are semantically strong but visually subtle.** Users may not discover that the rack, monitor, API diagram and backup objects are interactive.
4. **Prototype framing leaks into the presentation.** `v3-preview`, `scene / explorable` and game-like instructions make it feel more like a demo than a finished service surface.
5. **Several visual focal points compete.** Rack, monitor, window and desk lights have similar weight.
6. **Contrast is context-dependent.** HUD/copy over the illustration should use deliberate opaque or translucent surfaces rather than relying on the art alone.
7. **Mobile needs a dedicated pass.** Hover discovery cannot be the primary mechanism on touch; the readable service list should become a first-class navigation path.

## Recommended priority

### High

- Add a compact professional proposition in the first viewport: who Lautaro helps and what he stabilizes/automates.
- Add a persistent primary CTA such as `Discuss a system` or `Start a project` using a real configured destination, not a fabricated email.
- Add a visible `Explore the lab` affordance and a small hotspot legend/onboarding hint.
- Keep the same service areas available as direct buttons/links outside the image.

### Medium

- Replace or demote `v3-preview` in the primary HUD.
- Add a stronger backdrop behind HUD and any hero copy.
- Make the monitor the clear primary object through lighting/contrast and use the other hotspots as secondary.
- Add a touch/mobile mode where service buttons are visible without hover.

### Low

- Keep evening mode and outside transition as optional atmosphere features.
- Refine the generated depth map manually before production use.
- Consider adding one or two evidence-backed case links after the primary CTA is settled.

## Anti-slop score

**2/10**

- `default type`: partially present through system UI, but deliberate monospace/sans pairing reduces the issue.
- `center stack`: the underlying content is centered in places, but the scene composition is not a generic centered hero.

The important failure mode is not visual AI slop. It is conversion hierarchy: the experience is distinctive, but the offer and next action need to be promoted before polishing more atmosphere.

## Conclusion

The portfolio is visually viable as an original interactive technical portfolio. It should not be treated as final yet. The next iteration should be a conversion pass, not another decorative pass: visible proposition, real CTA, discoverable hotspots, and a touch-friendly direct path.
